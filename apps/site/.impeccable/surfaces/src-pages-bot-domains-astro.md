# Bot domains page

- Scope and mode: a public, dated resource page at `/bot-domains`; Read with a
  small Persuade action.
- Audience and job: people arriving from social posts want to scan available
  `.bot` names quickly, compare standard and premium prices, and open a
  registrar result.
- Action and proof: the full reviewed list stays public. The page shows the
  check date, source, price source, and direct registrar links. Newsletter
  signup is the next step for readers who want future finds.
- Direction: use Ian.is visual language with the dense, divided list structure
  of Brian Lovin's Sites page. The memorable moment is the list beginning in
  the first viewport, with quiet filters and no card grid.
- Constraints: static data, no live registrar calls, no signup gate, useful
  mobile rows, clear premium labels, and an explicit availability warning.
